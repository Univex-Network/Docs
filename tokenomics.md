## 🔒 Vesting & Safeguard Mechanism

- **Team Allocation (15%):** Backed by a strict 24-month hard lock cliff to guarantee absolute alignment with long-term network growth.
- **Liquidity Pool (15%):** 100% locked upon initial DEX/CEX pool launch to prevent slippage and establish trust.
- **Ecosystem & Grants (20%):** Distributed linearly over 24 months to support dApp developers, third-party node validators, and AI engineers.
- **Community Rewards (45%):** Minted dynamically via mobile node telemetry and testnet consensus activity.
